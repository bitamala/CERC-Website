Training to Transform: CERC and ACAJ Lead Anti-Corruption Efforts in Kinshasa

From December 4 to 6, 2024, CERC and the Association Congolaise pour l'Accès à la Justice (ACAJ) organized a three-day strategic training workshop in Kinshasa. The event trained civil society actors on responsible reporting, citizen oversight, and fostering a culture of integrity. Participants included community leaders, journalists, and youth advocates.

Dec 2024

Integrity Clubs Expand to 15 Schools Across Three Provinces

CERC's school integrity club program has grown significantly, now reaching students in Kinshasa, South Kivu, and Kasaï. Students are being trained to identify, document, and safely report corruption in their schools and communities, building the next generation of anti-corruption advocates.

Sep 2024

Community Monitors Complete Annual Training Cycle

More than 200 community monitors completed their annual refresher training, focusing on updated methodologies for tracking public infrastructure projects and health facility funding in rural areas. The monitors report findings to local authorities and media partners.

Jul 2024

CERC Joins International Anti-Corruption Alliance

CERC has strengthened its international presence by affiliating with a network of civil society organizations fighting corruption in sub-Saharan Africa. This partnership enables sharing of research tools, training methodologies, and cross-border advocacy strategies.

Mar 2024

New Research Report: Public Budget Transparency in DRC Education Sector

CERC released its latest research report examining budget transparency in DRC's education ministry. The report documents cases of fund diversion, proposes policy reforms, and provides recommendations for civil society engagement in budget monitoring.
