Who we are

About CERC

The Centre de Recherche sur l'Anti-Corruption is a Congolese organization promoting integrity, ethical leadership, and public accountability.

Mission

Advance anti-corruption knowledge and empower citizens to demand transparent and fair public services.

Core values

Integrity

We uphold ethical conduct in all interventions and partnerships.

Inclusion

We involve women, youth, and marginalized communities in decision-making.

Evidence

We use research and data to shape action and policy recommendations.
