Who we are

About CERC

The Centre de Recherche sur l'Anti-Corruption is an independent, non-profit Congolese organization promoting integrity, ethical leadership, and public accountability since its founding in Kinshasa.

Mission

Empower citizens — especially youth — to build integrity and stop corruption through education, civic engagement, training, and awareness-raising activities.

Vision & Values

Vision

A just Congo free from corruption, where public resources truly serve all citizens.

Integrity

We uphold ethical conduct in all our interventions, partnerships, and internal practices.

Inclusion

We involve women, youth, and marginalized communities in decision-making and program design.

Evidence

We use research and data to shape action, policy recommendations, and public debate.

Accountability

We hold ourselves and partner institutions to the same transparency standards we promote.

Collaboration

We build networks with civil society, international organizations, and government bodies to amplify impact.
