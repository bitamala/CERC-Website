What we do

Research

We produce evidence on corruption risks, public service integrity, and governance gaps across the DRC.

Training

We equip youth and communities with practical anti-corruption tools, civic skills, and accountability methodologies.

Advocacy

We promote policy and institutional reforms for transparent, fair, and inclusive governance in the DRC.
